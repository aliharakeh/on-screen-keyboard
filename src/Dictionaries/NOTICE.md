# Word lists

`words.en.txt` and `words.ar.txt` are the top 30,000 entries of the 2018 English and Arabic
50k frequency lists from [FrequencyWords](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords) by Hermit Dave,
which are derived from OpenSubtitles. Entries containing digits, symbols or (for English) non-ASCII
letters were removed, counts were dropped (line order is frequency rank), and English contraction
fragments ("don" + "t") were rejoined into words such as "don't" and "I'm".
